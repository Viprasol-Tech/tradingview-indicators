# Contributing

These scripts are mirrored from the [Viprasol profile on TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/u/viprasol/).
TradingView is where they are published and updated; this repo keeps the source in one place and
tracks problems.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue and include:

- the script name
- the symbol and timeframe
- what you saw and what you expected
- a screenshot of the chart, with the script's settings if you changed them

Repainting, signals that move after the bar closes, and compile errors are all worth reporting.

## Proposing a change

Pull requests are welcome for bug fixes.

- Change one script per pull request.
- Keep the file's header, licence line and credits as they are.
- Say what you tested: symbol, timeframe, and what changed on the chart.

An accepted fix is also published to TradingView by Viprasol, so the two stay the same.
